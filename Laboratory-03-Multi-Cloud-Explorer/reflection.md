# Cloud Computing Mission Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is **AWS** because it offers many services for different types of projects. I like that AWS can support both small startups and large companies. Its flexibility and scalability make it a useful choice for businesses that want to grow.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and identity management. They also allow businesses to use cloud resources without buying and maintaining their own physical hardware. However, each platform has its own strengths, such as Azure's strong connection with Microsoft products and GCP's focus on AI, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes.

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform because every company has different needs. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft products may choose Azure, while an AI research company may prefer GCP. Businesses also need to consider their budget, security, performance, scalability, and future growth.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me how to match cloud services with real business needs. I learned that choosing a cloud provider is not only about finding the platform with the most services. It is important to understand the client's situation and recommend a platform that fits their goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because I added more organized and useful work. I included cloud comparison tables, client recommendations, service comparisons, and a multi-cloud decision matrix. These activities helped me understand AWS, Azure, and GCP better and improved my confidence in making cloud recommendations.
